The Cyprus University of Technology is committed to making this website accessible, in accordance with EU Directive 2016/2102 on the accessibility of the websites of public sector bodies.

Compliance status

The website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. It has not yet been fully evaluated.

What we do

  • The site can be used with the keyboard only.
  • Text and background colours are chosen for sufficient contrast.
  • Images have text alternatives.
  • The layout adapts to phones, tablets and 200% zoom.
