HEAL brings together academic research, a mobility operator and the city. The formal consortium is CUT (Host Organisation) and NextBike Cyprus (Partner 1). Limassol Municipality supports the project as end user.

[heal_consortium]

Cyprus University of Technology

The project is led within CUT by the Cyprus International Institute for Environmental and Public Health (CII) and its Precision Exposomics and Health Laboratory (PEHL), which brings expertise in digital health, behavioural science and Just-in-Time Adaptive Interventions.
