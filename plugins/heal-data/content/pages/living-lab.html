Limassol is one of the 100 EU cities selected for the EU Mission on climate-neutral and smart cities by 2030. HEAL aims to help make the city a "living lab" where public health and urban sustainability support each other.

Why Limassol

Cycling links personal health with the city's climate goals. In a congested city centre, active travel can also be practical: for a typical 3 km trip from the Old Port to the municipal administrative centre, a bicycle can be faster door to door than a car once parking is included.

The green route

The intervention focuses on the Limassol cycling green route and the area it encloses, where sustainable mobility, urban greening and health promotion are priorities.

A map of the route will be added once permission is granted by NextBike and Limassol Municipality.

Who is involved

  • Cyprus University of Technology (CUT) leads the research.
  • NextBike Cyprus operates the public bike-sharing system, with more than 2,000 bicycles across Cypriot cities. It supplies the verified ride data.
  • Limassol Municipality supports the project as end user and stakeholder with a Letter of Support, including outreach to citizens.
